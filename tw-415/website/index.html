How this number is calculated

The concept: expected lost revenue. Every missed call is demand that reached you and got nothing back. This prices the jobs you almost won.

leak / month = missed calls / week × 4.33 × avg job value × close rate

Why it works: your close rate says what share of those callers you would have won. The job value says what each was worth. 4.33 is the average number of weeks in a month. Multiply the three and the leak stops being a feeling and becomes a figure you can act on.
